# v0.3 plan — LED-bar indicator panel (TTL)

Status: **planned, not started.** Decided 2026-09-08; <span style="color:#0040a0">carry-over fixes from the 2026-10-19 design review added
(section below, blue).</span> v0.2.x is the board to fabricate and test first; what is learned there (bus timing on
the real EI, brightness, the three measurements, TEST_PLAN 3.3.7) feeds this.

## Decision
- Stay TTL (no microcontroller). Rationale: an indicator panel, deterministic capture of µs pulses,
  no firmware to bring up. If a track display or PC logging is ever wanted, that is a different board
  sharing this one's J1/J2/protection front end.
- Replace the eight discrete LEDs with **four 8-bit LED bar modules, one per drive** (Ven has the
  Amazon B0GT4DWRX1 4-pack: "3-24V 8 Bit LED Indicator Module, Common Cathode, Blue & Red";
  22.86 × 14.13 mm, 8 SMD LEDs with on-board resistors, 9-pin 0.1 in header D1–D8 + COM).

## Module facts that drive the design
| Fact | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Common **cathode** | COM to GND; an input driven **high** lights the LED → drivers must source |
| On-board ≈ 2 kΩ per LED (this family; confirm marking "202") | needs a 5 V CMOS output swing: 74LS VOH ≈ 3.4 V gives 0.3–0.75 mA (too dim); **74HCT** gives 1–1.5 mA |
| 9-pin header | four 9-pin female headers along the front edge; ≈ 23 mm per module |
| 4-pack "Blue & Red" | colour split per module to be confirmed by Ven; decides one colour per drive vs mixed |

## Architecture
```
bus line (active-low) ─┬─ 470R ─ 74HCT14 ─ active-high ─┬─ 74HCT244 #1 (EN = DS1 node, low when selected) ─ module 1
                       └─ 100k to +5V  (pull-up on the FINGER side, design review F1)
                                                       ├─ 74HCT244 #2 (EN = DS2 node) ─ module 2
                                                       ├─ 74HCT244 #3 (EN = DS3 node) ─ module 3
                                                       └─ 74HCT244 #4 (EN = DS4 node) ─ module 4
STEP, INDEX ─ 74LS123 dual monostable (≈ 40 ms) ─ Q (active-high) ─ same 244 inputs
SEL bit: 244 input tied high via 1k → lights whenever that drive's buffer is enabled
```
- Eight inputs are shared by all four buffers; the enables do the per-drive gating.
- Not selected → outputs tri-state → module dark (common cathode, no pull-ups).
- <span style="color:#0040a0">Receivers are 74HCT14 (was 74LS14) and the pull-ups sit on the finger side of the series resistor — see
  the carry-over section. HCT14 outputs drive the HCT244 inputs directly.</span>

## Bit assignment per module (D1 → D8)
| Bit | Signal | Source | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SEL | tied high | on whenever selected |
| 2 | WRITE GATE | host, level | today's green |
| 3 | MOTOR ON | host, level | |
| 4 | STEP | host, pulse | via 74LS123 |
| 5 | DIRECTION | host, level | meaningful during STEP |
| 6 | TRACK 00 | selected drive | |
| 7 | WRITE PROTECT | selected drive | |
| 8 | INDEX | selected drive, pulse | via 74LS123, ≈ 5 Hz blink = disk turning |

## Parts delta vs v0.2.4
- **Remove:** U2 74LS00, U3 74LS06, D1–D8, R1–R8, their footprints and the SEL/WR silk.
- **Add:** 4 × 74HCT244 (DIP-20), <span style="color:#0040a0">2 × 74HCT14 (11 receivers in total; replaces U1's 74LS14 too)</span>, 1 × 74LS123
  (DIP-16) + 2 × RC (e.g. 47 kΩ / 1 µF ≈ 33 ms), 4 × 9-pin female header 0.1 in, 6 × 470 Ω and
  6 × 10 kΩ for the new lines (MOTOR ON pin 16, STEP 20, DIRECTION 18, TRACK 00 26, WRITE PROTECT 28,
  INDEX 8), 100 nF per new IC<span style="color:#0040a0">; 1 × 1N4148 in series with D12; pull-ups become 100 kΩ (11 of them)</span>.
- **Unchanged:** J1, J2 (component side, right-angle, as settled in 0.2.4 — single-sided PCBA then
  populates everything), F1/Q1/D11/C6/C4, D12/D13 indicators, R14–R18, outline, mounting.
  <span style="color:#0040a0">U1 becomes a 74HCT14 and R9–R13 move to the finger side (below).</span>

## Protection carries over
Every new bus line enters through 470 Ω exactly as the five existing ones; the EI fault analysis in
THEORY §4.2 applies unchanged. Input count rises from 5 to 11 taps<span style="color:#0040a0">, each ≈ 0.05 mA on the bus with the
100 kΩ pull-ups (was ≤ 0.6 mA)</span>.

## Carry-over fixes from the 2026-10-19 design review

<span style="color:#0040a0">All four come from the review recorded in `TEST_PLAN.md` §0.7; F1 is the one that matters.</span>

| # | 0.2.x problem | v0.3 change | Why it works |
|--|------------------------------|----------------------------|------------------------------|
| F1 | <span style="color:#0040a0">Low-level margin at the receiver: LS14 input current + the receiver-side 10 k pull-up both flow out through the 470 Ω, so a 0.4 V bus low can reach the input as ≈ 0.79 V (V_T− min 0.5 V). THEORY §3.0 (corrected).</span> | <span style="color:#0040a0">(a) Receivers become **74HCT14**. (b) Pull-ups move to the **finger side** of the 470 Ω and become **100 kΩ**.</span> | <span style="color:#0040a0">HCT input current is µA, so nothing drops across the 470 Ω; the pull-up's current goes into the terminated bus, not through the series resistor. The receiver then sees the bus voltage itself (≤ 0.4 V) against V_T− ≥ 0.5 V — the same margin a stock drive has. 100 k is enough to hold a CMOS input high with the cable unplugged and cuts the current into a powered-off EI to ≈ 50 µA per line. The 470 Ω fault bound (THEORY §4.2) is unchanged.</span> |
| F2 | <span style="color:#0040a0">+12 V on the +5 V pin: the cold PTC does not limit the current; D11 takes a hard pulse until F1 trips and may fail short.</span> | <span style="color:#0040a0">None — accepted.</span> | <span style="color:#0040a0">Any series element that would limit the pulse also costs voltage on a 74LS rail. The Mate-N-Lok is keyed; the fault needs a hand-made cable. A shorted D11 is fail-safe for the EI.</span> |
| F3 | <span style="color:#0040a0">Reversed +12 V drives D12 ≈ 2 V past its reverse rating.</span> | <span style="color:#0040a0">**1N4148 in series with D12** (cathode toward D12's anode).</span> | <span style="color:#0040a0">The 1N4148 takes the reverse voltage (rated 100 V); costs 0.7 V of the 10 V across R19, so D12 still gets ≈ 4 mA with R19 = 2k2.</span> |
| F4 | <span style="color:#0040a0">D11 5.6 V soft knee leaks a few mA at 5.25 V.</span> | <span style="color:#0040a0">Keep 1N5339B unless 2.4.2 shows > 10 mA at 5.25 V; then 1N5340B (6.0 V).</span> | <span style="color:#0040a0">6.0 V still clamps under the 74LS 7 V absolute maximum at the currents F1 allows; the trade is slightly more rail excursion in a fault for no leakage in normal use.</span> |

<span style="color:#0040a0">Not carried over: the 74LS37 note for U2 — v0.3 has no U2; the modules are driven by the HCT244s.</span>
